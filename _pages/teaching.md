I endorse these fundamental axioms desribed by [Federico Ardila](https://fardila.com/).

<u> Axiom 1</u> Mathematical potential is equally present in different groups irrespective of geographic, demographic, and economic boundaries.
<u> Axiom 2</u> Everyone can have joyful, meaningful, and empowering mathematical experiences.
<u> Axiom 3</u> Mathematics is a powerful, malleable tool that can be shaped and used differently by various communities to serve their needs.
<u> Axiom 4</u> Every student deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.
