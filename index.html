I am a graduate student in the Mathematics department at Syracuse University. I am advised by Prof. Graham Leuschke.

I am expected to graduate with my PhD in May 2026 and currently am on the job market.

My research interests are in commutative algebra. In particular, I like thinking about maximal Cohen-Macaulay (MCM) modules over Cohen-Macaulay rings.
